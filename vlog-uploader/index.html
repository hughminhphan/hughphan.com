vlog uploader

A personal tool operated by Hugh Phan to upload approved weekly vlogs to his own YouTube channel.

what it does

The tool requests permission to upload YouTube videos and view the connected YouTube account. It checks the channel identity before saving authorization and uses that authorization for the approved vlog upload workflow.

This is a private, owner-operated tool, not a public sign-up service. It is not made or endorsed by Google or YouTube.
