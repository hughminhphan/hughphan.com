vlog uploader / privacy

privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

This policy covers vlog uploader, a personal YouTube upload tool operated by Hugh Phan. Contact: hughphanman@gmail.com.

information accessed and used

With the owner's Google authorization, the tool accesses YouTube channel identity and account information to verify the connected channel. It uses video-upload permission to upload owner-approved vlog files and their accompanying metadata. It does not request Gmail, Google Drive, passwords, or unrelated Google account data.

storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's local password store and on the owner's reel-box upload machine so approved uploads can run without repeated sign-in. The workflow sends videos, metadata, and authorized API requests to Google/YouTube. The tool does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Access is limited to operating and maintaining the owner's upload workflow.

retention and control

Authorization credentials are retained while the integration is in use, until the owner removes or replaces them. Google access can be revoked at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself delete locally stored credentials or videos already uploaded to YouTube. Contact Hugh at the address above to request removal of locally stored integration data; manage uploaded videos in YouTube Studio.

Google API user data

vlog uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google/YouTube also process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

updates

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
